Down alternative duvets have come a long way from the scratchy, lumpy polyester blankets of the past. They offer the fluffy loft and cozy warmth of real down without the ethical concerns or allergy triggers, plus they’re machine-washable and often cost less. But not all budget-friendly inserts are created equal: some sleep hot, some lose their puff after a few washes, and others rustle like a trash bag. When shopping on a budget, focus on the fill material (microfiber or hollow-fiber vs. cheaper polyfill), the baffle-box construction (keeps fill evenly distributed), and the shell fabric (cotton or brushed microfiber for quietness and breathability). The three picks below represent the best value we’ve found after combing through owner reviews and expert tests, none of them will break the bank, but each serves a slightly different sleeper.
